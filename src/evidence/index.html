How this tool is sourced

Every printed statement, its cited source, and the passage that supports it.

Every clinical statement that can appear on the bedside tools is listed here with the source it cites, the evidence status, and the passage from that source that supports it — so you can check each statement against its reference without downloading the full text.

Reference aid only. This tool supports — and does not replace — clinical judgment, local protocol, and prescriber/pharmacy review. It is not a standalone order set or a validated decision-support device. Verify all medication decisions against current institutional policy.

About the quoted passages. Supporting passages are excerpted from their cited sources for citation verification and commentary, each attributed with a link to the original. Rights to the quoted text remain with the respective publishers. The full methodology, instrument logic, and citation registry are reproduced in the Methodology & clinical basis section below — also maintained as the clinical methodology document.
